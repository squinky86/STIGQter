The TippingPoint TPS must have FIPS mode enforced.

CCI(s)

  • CCI-003123 - Implement organization-defined cryptographic mechanisms to protect the confidentiality of nonlocal maintenance and diagnostic communications.

Weight

10

Fix Recommendation

Enable the SMS FIPS Mode:

1. Click Admin and Management.
2. Click Enable FIPS Mode by selecting Edit. This must be done in an approved change window since the SMS will reboot.

Check Contents

In the SMS client:

1. Click Admin and Management.
2. Ensure the SMS is in FIPS Mode.

If the SMS is not in FIPS mode, this is a finding.
