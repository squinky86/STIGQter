STIGQter: STIG Summary: Jamf Pro v10.x EMM Security Technical Implementation Guide Version: 3 Release: 1 Benchmark Date: 24 Jul 2024:

The Jamf Pro EMM local accounts must be configured with at least one uppercase character.

CCI(s)

  • CCI-004066 - For password-based authentication, enforce organization-defined composition and complexity rules.

Weight

10

Fix Recommendation

To configure the "Require uppercase character" of the local accounts password, do the following:

1. Open the Jamf Pro EMM console.
2. Click "Settings".
3. Click "System Settings".
4. Click "Jamf Pro System User Accounts & Groups".
5. Click "Password Policy".
6. Click "Edit".
7. Select "Require uppercase character".

Check Contents

To verify the "Require uppercase character" of the local accounts password is selected, do the following:

1. Open the Jamf Pro EMM console.
2. Click "Settings".
3. Click "System Settings".
4. Click "Jamf Pro System User Accounts & Groups".
5. Click "Password Policy".
6. Verify "Require uppercase character" is selected.

If "Require uppercase character" is not selected, this is a finding.
