Information Assurance - Authorizing Official (AO) and DODIN Connection Approval Office (CAO) Approval Documentation for use of KVM and A/B switches for Sharing of Classified and Unclassified Peripheral Devices

DISA Rule

SV-245784r1226312_rule

V-245784

Group Title

IA-10.03.01

Rule Version

IA-10.03.01

Severity

CAT III

CCI(s)

  • CCI-000366 - Implement the security configuration settings.

Weight

10

Fix Recommendation

1. The Enclave AO must specifically document the approval for use of KVM and/or A/B switches in the ATO or other official documentation signed by the AO authorizing use of switches between high-side (classified/SIPRNet) and low-side (unclassified/NIPRNet) shared devices.

2. The AO must submit initial and updated documentation (as required) to the DODIN CAO reflecting the use or addition of KVM or A/B devices on a user's enclave. The documentation may be part of the Authorization and Accreditation (A&A) documentation IAW RMF procedures or otherwise as specified by the DODIN CAO.

Check Contents

1. Check to ensure the Enclave AO has specifically documented the approval for use of KVM and/or A/B switches in the ATO or other official documentation signed by the AO authorizing use of switches between high-side (classified/SIPRNet) and low-side (unclassified/NIPRNet) shared devices.

2. Check to ensure the AO has submitted initial and updated documentation (as required) to the DODIN CAO reflecting the use or addition of KVM or A/B devices on a user's enclave. The documentation may be part of the Authorization and Accreditation (A&A) documentation IAW RMF procedures or otherwise as specified by the DODIN CAO.

TACTICAL ENVIRONMENT: The check is applicable where KVM devices are in use.
