STIGQter: STIG Summary: Virtual Private Network (VPN) Security Requirements Guide Version: 3 Release: 5 Benchmark Date: 01 Jul 2026:

The Remote Access VPN Gateway and/or client must enforce a policy to retain the Standard Mandatory DOD Notice and Consent Banner on the screen until users acknowledge the usage conditions and take explicit actions to log on for further access.

DISA Rule

Group Title

Severity

CAT II

CCI(s)

  • CCI-000050 - Retain the notification message or banner on the screen until users acknowledge the usage conditions and take explicit actions to log on to or further access the system.

Weight

10

Fix Recommendation

Configure the Remote Access VPN Gateway and/or client to retain the Standard Mandatory DOD-approved Notice and Consent Banner on the screen until users acknowledge the usage conditions and take explicit actions to log on for further access.

Check Contents

If the user/remote client connection banner is the same as the banner configured as part of the NDM SRG, this is not applicable.

Verify the ALG retains the Standard Mandatory DOD-approved Notice and Consent Banner on the screen until users acknowledge the usage conditions and take explicit actions to log on for further access.

If the Remote Access VPN Gateway and/or client does not retain the Standard Mandatory DOD-approved Notice and Consent Banner on the screen until users acknowledge the usage conditions and take explicit actions to log on for further access, this is a finding.
