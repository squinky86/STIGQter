STIGQter: STIG Summary: Riverbed NetProfiler Security Technical Implementation Guide Version: 2 Release: 1 Benchmark Date: 24 Jul 2024:

The Riverbed NetProfiler must be configured to retain the Standard Mandatory DOD Notice and Consent Banner on the screen until the administrator acknowledges the usage conditions and takes explicit actions to log on for further access.

CCI(s)

  • CCI-000050 - Retain the notification message or banner on the screen until users acknowledge the usage conditions and take explicit actions to log on to or further access the system.

Weight

10

Fix Recommendation

Go to Administration >> Account Management >> User Accounts.

Click "Settings".

Under "Log-in Settings", on the "Log-in splash screen display", use the drop-down menu to select "Show until Acknowledged".

Check Contents

Go to Administration >> Account Management >> User Accounts.

Click "Settings".

Check under "Log-in Settings".

If the "Log-in splash screen display" is not set to "Show until Acknowledged", this is a finding.
