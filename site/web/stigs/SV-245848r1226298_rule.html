Controlled Unclassified Information - Posting Only on Web-Sites with Appropriate Encryption; not on Publicly Accessible Web-Sites.

Weight

10

Fix Recommendation

Ensure the following standards/guidance are adhered to:

1. PII and other CUI may NOT be posted to publicly accessible internet sites and may NOT be posted to sites whose access is controlled only by domain (e.g., limited to .mil and/or .gov) as such restricted access can easily be circumvented.

2. At a minimum, posting CUI to a website requires certificate-based (e.g., common access card) or password and ID access as well as encrypted transmission using https or similar technology.
