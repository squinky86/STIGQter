STIGQter: STIG Summary: HP FlexFabric Switch NDM Security Technical Implementation Guide Version: 1 Release: 4 Benchmark Date: 02 Jul 2025:

The HP FlexFabric Switch must notify the administrator, upon successful logon (access), of the location of last logon (terminal or IP address) in addition to the date and time of the last logon (access).

Weight

Fix Recommendation

Configure the HP FlexFabric Switch to notify the administrator upon successful logon of the location of last logon (terminal or IP address).

[HP] password-control enabled

Check Contents

Determine if the HP FlexFabric Switch notifies the administrator upon successful logon of the location of last logon (terminal or IP address) in addition to the date and time of the last logon.

[HP] display password-control

Global password control configurations:
Password control: Enabled

If the administrator is not notified of the location of last logon (terminal or IP address) upon successful logon, this is a finding.
