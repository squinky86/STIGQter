The Session Border Controller (SBC) must be configured to validate the structure and validity of SIP and AS-SIP messages so that malformed messages or messages containing errors are dropped before action is taken on the contents.

Fix Recommendation

Ensure the DISN NIPRNet IPVS SBC is configured to validate the structure and validity of SIP and AS-SIP messages so that malformed messages or messages containing errors are dropped before action is taken on its contents.

NOTE: The VVoIP system may allow SIP and SRTP traffic encrypted and encapsulated on port 443 from cloud service providers.

Check Contents

Verify the DISN NIPRNet IPVS SBC is configured to validate the structure and validity of SIP and AS-SIP messages so that malformed messages or messages containing errors are dropped before action is taken on the contents.

If the SBC does not validate the correct format of the received AS-SIP message, this is a finding.

NOTE: The VVoIP system may allow SIP and SRTP traffic encrypted and encapsulated on port 443 from cloud service providers.
